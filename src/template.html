Focus

Our priorities for the launch.
  1. Win SEA and Mainland Chinese tourists. Bring them into the shop through our SEA creators, and through 小紅書 (Xiaohongshu) KOLs and social media pages.
  2. Ride the Lee Gardens Winter Fest. Turn the festival buzz on our doorstep into visits to Grade10.
  3. Make the most of the booths outside our door. Use the Winter Fest stalls in front of the shop to steer foot traffic inside.
  4. Open before the Winter Fest. The grand opening is now 6 Dec, so it doesn't clash with the Winter Fest opening on 11 Dec in front of Hysan Place and our shop.

Lee Gardens Winter Fest

The festival takes over Hysan Place, Pak Sha Road and the surrounding streets, right outside our shop. We plan to turn its crowds into our customers.
