Focus

Where the launch effort goes now.
  1. Capture SEA and Mainland Chinese tourists. Bring both into the shop, with the SEA creators below and China KOL visits on WeChat and XHS.
  2. Ride the Lee Gardens Winter Fest. Use the festival hype on our doorstep to draw its crowds to Grade10.
  3. Use the booths outside Grade10. Turn the Winter Fest stalls in front of the shop into foot traffic for us.
  4. Launch before the Winter Fest opens. Bring the launch forward to 6 Dec or 10 Dec, so it doesn't clash with the Winter Fest opening on 11 Dec in front of Hysan and our shop.
  5. Move the early dates. The Pokémon car activation now starts 28 Nov, and the media announcement moves to 4 Dec or 8 Dec.
